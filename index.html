FAQs

Questions? We've got answers.

Can't find what you're looking for? Our patient helpdesk is available 24x7.

Call Helpdesk
How do I book an appointment?

Use the online booking form on this page, call +91 98765 43210, WhatsApp us, or walk in to the OPD registration desk. Online bookings get a priority digital token.

Do you accept health insurance / cashless treatment?

Yes. We have tie-ups with 150+ insurers and TPAs including CGHS, ECHS and Ayushman Bharat. Our insurance desk helps with pre-authorisation, usually within 30 minutes.

What are the visiting hours?

General wards: 11 AM – 1 PM and 5 PM – 7 PM. ICU visits are limited to one attendant at 12 PM and 6 PM for infection control.

How can I get my lab reports?

Reports are sent by SMS/WhatsApp and can be downloaded from the "Download Reports" option using your UHID and registered mobile number.

Do you provide treatment for international patients?

Yes. Our International Patient Desk assists with medical visas, cost estimates, airport transfers, accommodation and interpreters in 12 languages.

Is parking available?

Yes, we have a multi-level parking for 800+ vehicles with free valet for senior citizens and differently-abled patients.
