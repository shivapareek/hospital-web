Image and model credits

Every image on this site is a real photograph or a render of real medical or scientific data. None of it is AI-generated. Scans come from public research datasets and are shown for illustration; they are not CareNova patients. Photographs of rooms and equipment are from other facilities and are illustrative.

Licence
Public domain, as stated in the three.js examples/models/nrrd/README.txt and the imageio documentation ("to the best of our knowledge … public domain"). No attribution is required; the credit is a courtesy.
Changes
Converted from float32 (0..1) to uint8 (0..255). The conversion is lossless, because the source values are in steps of 1/32.
